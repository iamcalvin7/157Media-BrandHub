## WHO CHILIZ IS
Chiliz is the leading blockchain provider for 
the sports and entertainment industry. It powers 
Fan Tokens™ and the Socios.com platform, 
connecting fans to clubs and athletes they love 
through tokenised experiences.

## TONE OF VOICE

DO:
- Be bold and direct
- Use active voice and strong declarative statements
- Lead with the fan and the sport — not the technology
- Reference the community as partners
- Use specific numbers and data points
- Speak in present tense where possible
- Talk about outcomes first, technology second
- Always say "fans" — never "supporters"
- Always use "Fan Tokens™" with the trademark symbol

DON'T:
- Use passive, corporate, or hedging language
- Lead with blockchain — lead with sport, fans, and outcomes
- Say "crypto" alone without context
- Use investment-forward language
- Overcomplicate — explain complex ideas simply
- Use hollow closers: "future of sport", "changing the game", "revolutionising fandom"
- Use "Fan Tokens" without the ™ symbol

Example of the Chiliz voice:
"This isn't a theory, it's already happening. 
The roadmap is set. The execution starts now. 
This is SportFi. This is Chiliz 2030."

## WRITING RULES
- Anchor copy to real numbers and real moments
- For partnerships: tag partners with their @handle
- For renewals: reference past achievements before announcing what is next
- Final paragraph must introduce something new — never restate the opener
- For multi-component upgrades: use numbered section architecture
- Never reference competition IP not officially partnered

## LOGO RULES
- Always use the official Chiliz wordmark
- Approved backgrounds: Chiliz Midnight (#1D0238), White (#FFFFFF)
- Never recolour, stretch, or rotate the logo
- Minimum clear space on all sides equal to the height of the wordmark
