CONTENT:

## Announcement

Socios.com announced a landmark entry into U.S. sports through Fan Token™ partnerships with five major university athletic programs:

- Louisiana State University, LSU
- University of Maryland
- Michigan State University
- Penn State University
- Texas A&M University

The agreements were completed by Fan Token Management, FTM US, part of The Chiliz Group, in partnership with Playfly Sports.

The release describes these as the first Fan Token™ partnerships of this kind in the United States and the first deployment of Fan Tokens™ in college sports.

## Expansion plan

Socios.com plans to expand the programme to 30 university athletic departments over the next 12 months.

The release says this scale-out is intended to create network effects across collegiate Fan Tokens™, similar to the engagement dynamics that make NCAA athletics culturally powerful.

## Product and fan engagement

Fan Tokens™ are described as utility tokens that allow fans to support their schools’ athletic programmes and access new engagement experiences, rewards, and benefits.

Through Fan Tokens™, supporters may be able to:

- vote on fan-focused decisions
- participate in exclusive experiences
- unlock unique benefits
- connect with university teams in new ways

The release also says the emerging college Fan Token™ ecosystem will include performance-tied token supply gamification, creating a sentiment marketplace for fans.

## NIL and revenue model

A meaningful proportion of revenue generated from Fan Token™ sales will support student-athletes through Name, Image and Likeness, NIL, programmes.

The release states that these NIL activations will be administered by Playfly Sports in compliance with NCAA and conference rules.

Socios.com’s role is to contribute Fan Token™ revenue. Socios.com does not operate the NIL fund.

This is an important distinction for compliance and copywriting.

## U.S. regulatory context

The release references joint SEC and CFTC guidance from March 2026, stating that Socios.com was cited as evidence and that Fan Tokens™ were categorised as digital collectibles and digital tools.
